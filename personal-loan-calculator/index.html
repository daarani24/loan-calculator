How this personal loan calculator works

A personal loan is usually repaid in equal monthly payments, called EMIs, over a fixed term. You enter the loan amount, the yearly interest rate and the term. The calculator then shows your monthly payment, the total interest, the total you will pay and a full payment schedule. You can also add an extra monthly payment to see how much sooner you could finish.

Most personal loans are unsecured. That means nothing, such as a house or a car, is pledged against the loan. Because of this, personal loan rates are often higher than rates on secured loans, so the total interest matters a lot. Check it before you borrow.

A worked example

Take a loan of ₹3,00,000 at 12% a year for 3 years. These are the starting values in the ₹ INR calculator above.

  • The EMI is about ₹9,964.
  • Over 36 months you pay about ₹3.59 lakh in total. About ₹58,700 of that is interest.
  • In the first month, the interest is ₹3,00,000 × 12% ÷ 12, which is ₹3,000. So about ₹6,964 of your first EMI reduces the loan.
  • Now type 2 in the term box. The EMI rises to about ₹14,122, but the total interest falls to about ₹38,900.

The shorter loan costs about ₹4,158 more each month, and it saves about ₹19,800 in interest.

Tips before you take a personal loan

  • Borrow only what you need. A smaller loan means less interest and a smaller monthly burden.
  • Choose the shortest term you can afford. A long term gives a small EMI but a larger total cost.
  • Compare offers. Put two loans side by side in the loan comparison calculator and compare total interest.
  • Check the real rate. If a lender quotes a flat rate, convert it with the flat rate to reducing rate converter first.
  • Ask about fees. Processing fees, insurance and early repayment charges are not included here.
  • Plan extra payments. The loan prepayment calculator shows how much interest an extra payment saves.
  • Use a regulated lender. Check that the lender is properly registered in your country before you sign.

Frequently asked questions

How is a personal loan EMI calculated?

The EMI comes from the loan amount, the yearly interest rate and the number of months. The calculator finds the equal monthly payment that repays the loan with interest by the last month.

Why are personal loan rates often higher than home or car loan rates?

A personal loan is usually unsecured, which means there is no house or car backing it. Lenders take more risk, so they often charge a higher rate. Your own rate depends on your lender and your situation.

What affects the interest rate I am offered?

Lenders often look at your credit history, income, existing loans, the loan amount and the term. Rates differ between lenders, so compare more than one offer.

Does this calculator include the processing fee?

No. It only uses the amount, rate and term you enter. Processing fees, insurance and taxes raise the real cost of a loan, so ask your lender for the full cost.

Can I repay a personal loan early?

Often yes, and extra payments shorten the loan and save interest. Some lenders charge a fee for early repayment, so check your loan agreement first.
