How this education loan calculator works

An education loan helps pay for fees, living costs and other study expenses. You enter the loan amount, the yearly interest rate and the repayment term. The calculator then shows your monthly payment, the total interest, the total you will pay and a full payment schedule. You can also add an extra monthly payment to see how much sooner you could finish.

Many education loans work differently from other loans in one way. Repayment may not start until your course ends, and interest can build up in the meantime. This calculator starts repayment straight away and does not model a study period, so treat the result as an estimate of the repayment phase. Ask your lender about its rules.

A worked example

Take a loan of ₹10,00,000 at 9.5% a year, repaid over 7 years. These are the starting values in the ₹ INR calculator above.

  • The EMI is about ₹16,344.
  • Over 84 months you pay about ₹13.73 lakh in total. About ₹3.73 lakh of that is interest.
  • In the first month, the interest is ₹10,00,000 × 9.5% ÷ 12, which is ₹7,916.67. So about ₹8,427 of your first EMI reduces the loan.
  • Now type 5 in the term box. The EMI rises to about ₹21,002, but the total interest falls to about ₹2.60 lakh.

The shorter loan costs about ₹4,658 more each month, and it saves about ₹1.13 lakh in interest.

Tips before you take an education loan

  • Ask about the study period. Find out whether interest is charged while you study, and whether you can pay it as you go. Paying it early keeps the balance from growing.
  • Borrow only what you need. A smaller loan means less interest to repay later.
  • Plan the repayment term. Check that the EMI fits a realistic starting income. A shorter term costs less in total, but the payment is higher.
  • Compare offers. Put two loans side by side in the loan comparison calculator and compare total interest.
  • Pay extra when you can. The loan prepayment calculator shows how much interest an extra payment saves, for example after your first salary.
  • Ask about fees and tax rules. Processing fees are not included here, and the tax treatment of education loan interest depends on your country, so check the current rules.

Frequently asked questions

How is an education loan EMI calculated?

The EMI comes from the loan amount, the yearly interest rate and the number of months of repayment. The calculator finds the equal monthly payment that repays the loan with interest by the last month.

What is a moratorium or grace period?

Many education loans give you a period, often while you study and for a short time after, before full repayment starts. The rules differ by lender. This calculator starts repayment straight away, so it does not model a moratorium.

Does interest build up while I am studying?

It can, depending on the lender and the loan. Some lenders charge interest from the first payout, and unpaid interest may be added to the loan. Ask your lender how it works for your loan.

Can I repay an education loan early?

Often yes, and extra payments shorten the loan and save interest. Some lenders charge a fee for early repayment, so check your loan agreement first.

Does this calculator include fees?

No. It only uses the amount, rate and term you enter. Processing fees, insurance and other charges raise the real cost of a loan, so ask your lender for the full cost.
