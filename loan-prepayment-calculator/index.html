How loan prepayment saves you money

Interest is charged every month on the balance you still owe. When you pay extra, that extra money goes straight to the balance. A lower balance means less interest next month, and less again the month after. Over many years this adds up, so the loan ends earlier and you pay less in total.

This calculator shows two things at once. You can add an extra amount to every monthly payment, and you can make one lump-sum prepayment in a month you choose. It then compares your loan with and without these payments: time to repay, total interest and interest saved. Your normal monthly payment stays the same, and the loan simply ends sooner.

A worked example

Take a loan of ₹25,00,000 at 9% a year for 20 years. These are the starting values in the ₹ INR calculator above.

  • The monthly payment is about ₹22,493.
  • Over 20 years you pay about ₹29 lakh in interest.
  • Now type 5,000 in the extra monthly payment box (typing is more exact than the slider here).
  • The loan now ends in about 12 years and 10 months, which is about 7 years and 2 months sooner.
  • You save about ₹11.8 lakh in interest.

That is a large saving for ₹5,000 a month, because the extra money starts working in the first month, when the balance is highest.

Extra monthly payment or one-time prepayment?

  • Extra monthly payment: a small amount added every month. It is easy to plan, and its effect builds up slowly.
  • One-time prepayment: a lump sum, for example from a bonus. The earlier you make it, the more interest it saves. Try moving the month slider to see this.
  • Both together: you can use both in this calculator to see the combined effect.

Tips before you prepay

  • Check for fees. Some loans charge a prepayment fee or have rules about how much you can prepay. This calculator does not include fees.
  • Ask how your lender applies it. Some lenders shorten the loan, and others lower the monthly payment. This calculator shows the first case.
  • Keep an emergency fund. Do not use up all your cash to prepay a loan.
  • Compare with other uses of the money. If your loan rate is low, another use of the money might make sense. The loan comparison calculator can help you compare two loan options.
  • Know your starting payment. The EMI calculator shows your normal monthly payment and full schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Does prepaying a loan save interest?

Yes. Interest is charged on the balance you still owe, so every extra payment lowers the balance and the interest charged in the months that follow.

Is it better to pay extra every month or make one lump sum?

Both reduce the balance. The earlier the money goes in, the more interest it saves, so a lump sum paid early saves more than the same amount paid later. A regular extra payment can be easier to manage.

Does my monthly payment change when I prepay?

In this calculator the monthly payment stays the same and the loan ends sooner. Some lenders instead lower the payment, so ask your lender how your loan treats a prepayment.

Do lenders charge a prepayment fee?

Some do, and the rules differ by lender and loan type. This calculator does not include any fee, so check your loan agreement before you prepay.

When do prepayments save the most?

Earlier prepayments save more, because the balance is largest in the early years. Before you prepay, check for fees and keep enough cash for emergencies.
