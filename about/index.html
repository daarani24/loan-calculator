About EMI Loan Tools

EMI Loan Tools is a free set of loan calculators. It is an independent project, and it is not a bank, a lender or a financial adviser.

What you can do here

  • Work out the monthly payment, total interest and total cost of a loan.
  • Convert a flat interest rate into the real reducing balance rate.
  • See how much interest and time you save with extra or one-time prepayments.
  • Compare two loans side by side.
  • Check car, personal, education and home loans in rupees, pounds or dollars.

How the numbers are worked out

The monthly payment for a fixed-rate loan uses the standard formula:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1)

Here P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (the yearly rate divided by 12) and n is the number of monthly payments. If the interest rate is 0%, the payment is simply the loan amount divided by the number of months.

Each month, interest is charged on the balance that is still owed, and the rest of the payment reduces that balance. The payment schedule on each page is built this way, month by month.

The flat rate converter works out a flat-rate loan's monthly payment first. It then finds the reducing balance rate that gives the same monthly payment.

What the results mean

The results are estimates for fixed-rate loans with equal monthly payments. A real loan offer can differ because of processing fees, insurance, taxes, rate changes, the day your payments fall due, or how your lender rounds. Some lenders also charge a prepayment fee, which these calculators do not include. Always read your loan agreement, and ask your lender for an exact figure before you decide.

Nothing on this site is financial advice. The starting numbers on the sliders are only examples. They are not offers or quotes from any lender.

Your numbers stay with you

All calculations run in your own browser. The loan details you type in are not sent to our server. Your choice of currency is remembered in your browser so you don't have to select it again. You can read more on the Privacy Policy page.
